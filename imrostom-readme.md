🛍️ Developing **Shopify Apps** and storefront integrations using the **Admin API**, **Storefront API**, **Webhooks**, and **GraphQL**, with a strong focus on synchronization, scalability, and merchant experience.

🌱 Continuously expanding my expertise in **Cloud Architecture**, **Docker**, **AWS**, **Google Cloud**, **DigitalOcean**, **CI/CD**, **Redis**, and **Nginx**.
